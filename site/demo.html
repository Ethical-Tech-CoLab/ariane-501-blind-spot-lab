When does more testing help?

Assume independent opportunities, each with probability p of both exercising and detecting failure. This calculator is separate from the snapshot controls above.

P(at least one detection) = 1 - (1 - p)n

Baseline profiles assume retained alignment, no containment, a faithful implementation, and a safety oracle. The weak oracle and idealized stub have detection p = 0 in this model. No browser random generator is used; these are analytic probabilities, not a replay of Python's seeded counts.
