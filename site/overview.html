01 / THE VERDICT

More tests cannot discover
a failure your test world excludes.

The disaster was testable and preventable.
Monte Carlo would not automatically have found it.

The inquiry identified feasible representative ground tests and described post-flight reproduction. Our toy experiment makes a narrower point: discovery depends on input support, the implementation path, and an oracle that recognizes failure.
